Question 6: Systemwide in fall 2025, which group had the HIGHER freshman admit rate: White or Hispanic/Latino(a)?
Answer: Hispanic/Latino(a)

Hispanic/Latino(a): 74.5% admit rate (41,458 admits from 55,624 applicants)  
White: 68.7% admit rate (26,368 admits from 38,390 applicants)
