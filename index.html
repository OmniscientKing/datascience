UC Admissions After the SAT/ACT Ban

Tracking systemwide freshman & transfer admissions by ethnicity, 2017–2025

Standardized test scores (SAT/ACT) were once a core input in University of California admissions decisions. A 2020 court injunction barred UC from considering SAT/ACT scores starting with the Fall 2021 cycle, and the policy was made permanent (test-blind) for the years that follow. That shift, layered on top of the COVID-19 disruption to testing access in 2020, creates a natural before/after comparison.

Driving Question: How did the removal of SAT/ACT requirements affect UC admission rates across different ethnic groups?
